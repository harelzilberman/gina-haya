## 3. Clear Explanation
Natural weed control uses prevention and timing: mulch to block light, spacing to reduce gaps, and early removal before weeds set seed.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Mulch soil  
2) Remove early (small roots)  
3) Plant densely  
4) Avoid bare soil

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Letting weeds seed  
- Bare soil exposure  
- Late removal

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Weeds reveal conditions—respond by improving the system.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Fewer weeds over time, easier maintenance.
