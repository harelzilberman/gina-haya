# Compost — The Foundation of Soil Life

**SEO Title:** Compost Guide  
**Meta Description:** Learn how to build rich compost and create the foundation of living soil.

## 1. Hook
Everything in the garden begins—and ends—with decomposition.
Compost is where waste becomes life again.

## 2. Visual Guide
- Hero: compost pile steaming lightly  
- Process: greens/browns layering → turning → finished compost  
- Macro: crumbly finished compost  
- Result: healthy plants

## 3. Clear Explanation
Compost is decomposed organic matter that becomes nutrient-rich and biologically active.
It improves structure, feeds microbes, and supports resilient plant growth.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Gather materials (greens/browns)  
2) Build pile with balance  
3) Maintain moisture (like a wrung sponge)  
4) Turn occasionally  
5) Use when dark and earthy

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Wrong ratio greens/browns  
- Too dry or too wet  
- Not enough aeration

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Compost is transformation, not disposal. It’s the heart of a living system.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Richer soil, better growth, self-sustaining cycles.
