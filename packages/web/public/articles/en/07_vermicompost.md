# Worm Composting — Turning Waste into Living Fertility

**SEO Title:** Vermicompost Guide  
**Meta Description:** Use worms to convert kitchen scraps into rich, living compost.

## 1. Hook
Some of the most powerful workers in your garden are silent.
Worms transform leftovers into one of the richest materials you can give your soil.

## 2. Visual Guide
- Hero: worm bin with bedding and scraps

- Macro: red worms and castings

## 3. Clear Explanation
Vermicomposting uses worms to convert organic waste into castings—nutrient-rich, microbially active material.
It improves structure, boosts biology, and feeds plants gently without burning.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Prepare bin (drainage + airflow)  
2) Add bedding (shredded paper, leaves)  
3) Add worms (e.g., red wigglers)  
4) Feed scraps (small amounts, balanced)  
5) Harvest castings when dark and crumbly

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Overfeeding → smells/pests  
- Too wet or too dry  
- Wrong inputs (oily/salty foods)

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Worms are a direct expression of soil vitality. Where they thrive, conditions are balanced.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Rich, dark compost; improved soil texture; steady plant growth.
