## 3. Clear Explanation
Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone in small, controlled amounts.
It reduces evaporation, saves water, and keeps moisture levels stable—especially useful in warm climates.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Install main line and emitters  
2) Position near root zones  
3) Set timing based on soil and season  
4) Check for clogs and adjust

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Poor spacing of emitters  
- Clogging  
- Wrong timing

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Consistency creates stability. Drip systems allow a steady rhythm that plants can rely on.

Efficient water use, reduced stress, healthier growth.
