# Companion Planting — Working With Nature

**SEO Title:** Companion Planting Guide  
**Meta Description:** Use plant relationships to improve growth, repel pests, and support biodiversity.

## 1. Hook
Plants do not grow alone.
They interact, support, and sometimes protect each other.

## 3. Clear Explanation
Companion planting pairs species that benefit each other—through pest deterrence, shading, or attracting beneficial insects.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Choose compatible plants  
2) Combine in beds  
3) Observe interactions  
4) Adjust combinations

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Random mixing  
- Ignoring spacing  
- Overcrowding

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Diversity builds resilience. Relationships matter.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Healthier plants, fewer pests, richer ecosystem.
