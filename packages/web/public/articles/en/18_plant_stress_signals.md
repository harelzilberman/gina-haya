# Plant Stress Signals — How to Read Your Garden

**SEO Title:** Plant Stress Guide

## 1. Hook
Plants communicate—learn to read them.

## 2. Visual Guide
- Yellowing, wilting, curling

## 3. Clear Explanation
Signals: yellowing (nutrient/water), wilting (water imbalance), curling (stress/pests).

## 4. Steps
1) Observe  
2) Identify pattern  
3) Check soil  
4) Adjust

## 5. Mistakes
Guessing without observation

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Observation is the primary tool.

## 7. Results
Early correction, healthier plants

WHAT: read signals

WHY: prevent damage
