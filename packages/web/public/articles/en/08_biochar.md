## 3. Clear Explanation
Biochar is a carbon-rich, porous material added to soil.
It improves structure, increases water and nutrient retention, and provides habitat for microbes.
Crucially, it should be “charged” before use so it carries life and nutrients, not just empty carbon.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Source biochar  
2) Charge (soak with compost/tea/manure)  
3) Mix lightly into topsoil  
4) Observe over seasons

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Using raw (uncharged) biochar  
- Applying too much  
- Expecting instant results

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Biochar acts like a memory in the soil—slow, deep, cumulative change rather than quick spikes.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Improved tilth, steadier moisture, longer-lasting fertility.
