## 3. Clear Explanation

Seaweed spray is a diluted liquid made from marine algae, usually used as a foliar feed or gentle tonic. What makes it valuable is not only minerals, but also compounds associated with stronger stress response and steadier growth.

It can help plants handle:
- transplant shock
- heat
- dry periods
- weak recovery after stress

A useful way to see it: seaweed spray is less like a heavy meal and more like a restorative drink.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1 — Choose a good product
- **Action:** Select a quality seaweed extract
- **Details:** Look for a clean product without unnecessary additives.
- **Signals:** Dark brown liquid with natural marine smell

### Step 2 — Dilute correctly
- **Action:** Mix with water
- **Details:** Follow label instructions or stay on the gentle side.
- **Signals:** Thin, sprayable liquid

### Step 3 — Spray the leaves
- **Action:** Apply a fine mist to upper and lower leaf surfaces
- **Details:** Morning or late afternoon is best.
- **Signals:** Light coating, not heavy dripping

### Step 4 — Repeat rhythmically
- **Action:** Reapply every 1–2 weeks
- **Details:** Especially useful during active growth or stress.
- **Signals:** Stronger new growth and richer color

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid

- **Spraying too strong:** Overconcentration can stress the leaf → dilute gently.
- **Spraying in heat:** Midday sun increases stress → spray in cool hours.
- **Using it too often:** More is not always better → work in a calm rhythm.
- **Expecting it to fix everything:** Foliar support does not replace healthy soil.

## 6. Biodynamic Insight

Seaweed spray fits a biodynamic garden because it supports vitality without forcing exaggerated growth. Apply it when the plant is receptive: during active leaf growth, after transplanting, or at the beginning of stress periods.

The key is observation. If the garden feels tired or weather-stressed, seaweed can be a gentle restorative gesture.

## 7. Results / Transformation

The plant often settles into itself.

Leaves become firmer.  
Color becomes deeper.  
New growth looks more confident.

In warm climates, that stability matters as much as growth.

## 8. Quick Summary

- **What to do:** Dilute seaweed extract and spray lightly on leaves
- **When to do it:** Every 1–2 weeks during active growth or mild stress
- **Why it works:** It supports resilience, mineral balance, and recovery
