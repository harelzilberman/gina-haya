## 3. Clear Explanation
Soil structure is the arrangement of particles into aggregates. Good structure holds water, allows air, and supports roots.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Observe texture  
2) Add organic matter  
3) Avoid compaction  
4) Keep soil covered

## 5. Mistakes
Over-tilling, walking on beds, leaving soil bare

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Structure is grown through life, not forced mechanically.

## 7. Results
Crumbly soil, deeper roots, balanced moisture
