# Crop Rotation — Why It Matters

**SEO Title:** Crop Rotation Guide  
**Meta Description:** Understand how crop rotation prevents soil depletion and reduces pests.

## 1. Hook
Planting the same crop again and again seems simple—
but the soil remembers.

## 3. Clear Explanation
Crop rotation changes plant families over time to prevent nutrient depletion and break pest cycles.
It balances what is taken and what is returned.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide
1) Group plants by family  
2) Plan rotation across seasons  
3) Avoid repeating same family in same spot  
4) Observe and adjust

## 5. Mistakes to Avoid
- Repeating same crop  
- No planning  
- Ignoring soil feedback

## 6. Biodynamic Insight
Rotation is rhythm—giving and receiving over time.

## 7. Results / Transformation
Balanced soil, fewer pests, steadier yields.
